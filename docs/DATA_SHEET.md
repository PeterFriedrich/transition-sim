## 2. Science 10

No Science 10 data sheet has been supplied. Two things the Unit A concept set
does not give are taken from the **Alberta Chemistry 30 Data Booklet** (updated
2010) as transcribed and checked in the sibling repo chemistry_sim
(`site/js/chem/elements-data.js`, its `docs/DATA_SHEET.md` §1.8). **The owner
has not confirmed this choice of source.**

- **Element names** for Z = 1–20. The booklet prints "aluminium"; the site uses
  the concept set's spelling, "aluminum".
- **Mass numbers** for Z = 1–20, used for the neutron count in a nucleus label:
  the booklet's atomic molar mass rounded to a whole number. The concept set
  gives only sodium's (11 p⁺, 12 n⁰), which this reproduces.
